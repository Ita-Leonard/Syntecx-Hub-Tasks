# 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Performance Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence solution built with **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze organizational sales performance across products, customers, cities, and states.

Designed with an executive-friendly interface, the dashboard provides a centralized view of business performance by monitoring revenue, orders, product demand, and shipping costs. Interactive filters and visualizations allow users to explore sales trends, identify top-performing regions, evaluate customer contributions, and uncover opportunities for business growth.

This project demonstrates the practical application of **data analytics**, **business intelligence**, and **data storytelling** to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that support strategic decision-making.

# 💼 Business Problem

Businesses generate vast amounts of transactional sales data daily, making it difficult to identify trends, monitor performance, and make timely decisions.

Without an interactive reporting solution, organizations often struggle to:

- Track overall sales performance.
- Identify high-performing products and categories.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Compare sales across different geographical locations.
- Monitor shipping costs and operational efficiency.
- Detect revenue trends over time.

This dashboard addresses these challenges by providing an intuitive and interactive reporting platform for business stakeholders.
